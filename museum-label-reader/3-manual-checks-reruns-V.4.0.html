Labels to read

Which labels parse 2 and parse 3 read: all of them, or a number taken either first by file name or at random. Count single labels (crops), or specimens with all their labels. Check for contradictions

          
          
          
        

      Sends the prompt and your notes per column to the model chosen under Model and key on the left (one paid request). It lists instructions that contradict each other, or that a model could follow in conflicting ways, quoting the lines involved, for example a note that says to expand abbreviations while the prompt says to copy text exactly. It only flags them: nothing changes until you edit a quoted line and press Save edits, or press Fix and then Apply.
